About

I'm a Computer Science student at Universidade Federal de Viçosa (UFV), Campus Florestal, currently in my fourth semester. Before this, I completed a technical degree in Information Technology at the same campus, integrated with high school — so by the time I started my bachelor's, I already had a foundation in programming and a first real project shipped.

Right now most of my time outside classes goes into two things: building the institutional website for SetApp, UFV's Junior Enterprise, and working with a group of more senior Computer Science students on an educational game for local 4th-graders. Both have pushed me to work inside a team's existing codebase and conventions, rather than starting from a blank file.

I'm also interested in how AI-assisted tools change the way developers learn and debug — not as a shortcut, but as something worth understanding well. I recently co-instructed a Git & GitHub workshop for IT students, which is the kind of teaching-by-doing I'd like to keep making time for.
